FOOTBALL / SKILL LAB

Build your game.
One skill at a time.

Click any skill to open a short explanation and a drill you can try on the field.

01First touch & ball control＋

Relax your foot as the ball arrives and cushion it into the space you want to move next. Try to make your first touch useful, not just stop the ball.

  • Pass against a wall and control the return.
  • Repeat 10 times with each foot.
  • Try to direct your first touch left, right, and forward.
02Short passing＋

Use the inside of your foot for a controlled pass. Place your standing foot beside the ball and point it toward your teammate.

  • Stand 5–8 metres apart.
  • Pass to your partner’s feet, then receive with a soft first touch.
  • Gradually increase distance while keeping accuracy.
03Dribbling & changing direction＋

Keep the ball close with small touches, use both feet, and look up between touches to notice open space.

  • Place 4–5 cones or markers in a line.
  • Weave through them using inside and outside touches.
  • Practise an inside-foot cut to change direction quickly.
04Shooting basics＋

Look at your target, plant your non-kicking foot beside the ball, and keep your ankle firm. Focus on accuracy before power.

  • Start close to goal and aim for a corner.
  • Use the inside of your foot for placement or your laces for power.
  • Practise with both feet when safe and comfortable.
05Defending & teamwork＋

Stay balanced, bend your knees slightly, and guide the attacker away from dangerous space instead of diving in. Communicate with teammates.

  • Practise shadowing a partner at walking speed.
  • Keep a safe distance and watch the ball.
  • Call clearly when you need support.
20-minute practice plan
  1. 3 min — warm-up and movement
  2. 5 min — first touch and passing
  3. 5 min — dribbling through markers
  4. 5 min — controlled shooting
  5. 2 min — cool down and stretch
