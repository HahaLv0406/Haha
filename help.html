Football skill guide

Here are a few simple skills we can practise together. Start slowly, focus on control, and build up speed when the movement feels comfortable.

01 — First touch & ball control ＋

Keep your body relaxed and watch the ball arrive. Cushion the ball with the inside of your foot, then guide it into the space where you want to move next.

  • Pass against a wall and control the return with one touch.
  • Try 10 repetitions with each foot.
  • Challenge: control the ball so your second touch can move forward.
02 — Short passing ＋

Plant your non-kicking foot beside the ball, point it toward your target, and pass with the inside of your foot. Follow through toward your teammate.

  • Stand 5–8 metres apart.
  • Pass back and forth, aiming at your partner’s feet.
  • Move a little farther apart when your passes become accurate.
03 — Dribbling & changing direction ＋

Use small touches with both feet. Keep the ball close enough to reach in one step, and look up between touches so you can see the space around you.

  • Set up 4–5 cones or markers.
  • Dribble through them using the inside and outside of both feet.
  • Practise a simple inside-foot cut to change direction.
04 — Shooting basics ＋

Look at the target, place your standing foot beside the ball, and keep your ankle firm. Use the inside of your foot for placement or your laces for a stronger shot.

  • Begin close to goal and focus on accuracy.
  • Try aiming for the corners rather than shooting as hard as possible.
  • Practise with both feet when safe and comfortable.
05 — Defending & teamwork ＋

Stay balanced, bend your knees slightly, and slow the attacker down instead of diving in. Communicate with teammates and protect the space between you and the goal.

  • Practise shadowing a partner at walking speed first.
  • Keep a safe distance and watch the ball and the player.
  • Call clearly when you need support.
A simple 20-minute practice
  1. 3 min — gentle warm-up and movement
  2. 5 min — first touch and passing
  3. 5 min — dribbling through markers
  4. 5 min — controlled shooting
  5. 2 min — cool down and stretch

Remember: Warm up first, use a safe space, take water breaks, and practise at a pace that feels right. The goal is to improve step by step and enjoy playing together.
